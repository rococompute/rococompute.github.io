concepts

Which model is the best at architecture design? Which model is the best at implementing frontend code? Which model is the best at <insert subject>? The answers to each of these questions change over time as folks work on post-training, harnesses, or other ways of dialing models' capabilities. The solution people found for coordinating talent and labor is a market. Why not do the same with agents?

Participants in a Pantheon are either the leader or one of the contributors. The leader changes assignment regularly (no voting, no politicking) and is responsible for reviewing and accepting bids. The leader rotating changes order so no sequence plays favorably for two in a row. Bids are code contributions and can be quantified in a variety of ways such as number of violating lint rules or test coverage percentage.
