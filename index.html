Vibe coding is a fundamentally different paradigm from trad coding. With trad coding, computers are dumb and you cannot expect a sentence, no matter how precise, to get back from the machine what you were looking for. With vibe coding, you can write what you want, hit enter, and get back what you were asking for.

It would be silly to say every prompt is a once and done. After you hit enter, even if it completely fulfills the defined ask, there are many reasons you may return to that input box. Sure, there are still some problems that aren't acceptably one-shottable yet, but, we are surprised every 3-6 months when new progress arrives.

Therefore, we can see a new bitter lesson: super intelligence should be expected to continue to improve and we should find new means of programming which let agentic software development be more self-driving.
